Get to know our farm
Welcome to the farm

Good things grow from small beginnings.

At SW Michigan Garlic LLC, we're building a specialty garlic farm one season at a time in the countryside of Southwest Michigan.

Our journey begins with approximately 500 cloves planted in fall 2026 for the 2027 growing season. Rather than rushing to market, we're carefully growing and saving our harvest to expand healthy planting stock for future seasons.

We believe patience, careful growing practices, and a connection to the land are worth cultivating.

What we're growing

Two kinds of garlic. One growing dream.

01 / OUR MAIN CROP

Hardneck Garlic

Known for its distinctive scapes, rich flavor, and beautiful cloves. Hardneck garlic is the heart of our developing seed-stock operation.

Approximately 475 cloves planned for fall 2026 planting.

02 / A SPECIALTY FAVORITE

Elephant Garlic

Impressive, oversized bulbs with a milder flavor. We're growing a small selection alongside our hardneck crop as we learn and expand.

Approximately 25 cloves planned for fall 2026 planting.

Growing now, not selling yet. We're currently reserving our harvest for replanting and building future seed stock. Product availability and sales dates will be announced when we're ready.
